ABOUT ROAB

The Research Of Abnormal Behaviour (ROAB) Foundation is a premier research institution dedicated to studying unusual and atypical behavioral patterns across multiple domains of human experience.

Founded in 1952, ROAB maintains a comprehensive database of behavioral anomalies, psychological phenomena, and documented cases of unusual human responses to environmental stimuli.

Our mission is to advance understanding of human behavior through rigorous scientific methodology and peer-reviewed research.
