ABOUT ROAB

The Research Of Abnormal Behaviour (ROAB) Foundation is a premier research institution dedicated to the scientific study of anomalous behaviour patterns, atypical psychological phenomena, and unusual biological responses to environmental conditions.

Established by Ivo Newton, ROAB operates as an independent research organization with departments focused on behavioral analysis, psychological investigation, biological research, and phenomenological documentation. Our work spans theoretical research, field investigation, and applied behavioral studies.

ROAB maintains comprehensive documentation of anomalous cases, behavioral incidents, and unusual phenomena reported across multiple disciplines. Our research contributes to the broader scientific understanding of human behavior, psychological response patterns, and unexplained biological phenomena.

RESEARCH DEPARTMENTS

BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS

Study of abnormal behavioral patterns, psychological anomalies, and atypical human responses to controlled and uncontrolled environmental stimuli.

BIOLOGICAL RESEARCH

Investigation of unusual biological phenomena, physiological responses, and documented cases of atypical biological adaptation.

PHENOMENOLOGY

Documentation and analysis of unusual phenomena, anomalous incidents, and cases that fall outside established behavioral frameworks.

SECURITY & CONTAINMENT

Personnel safety protocols, facility security, incident response, and management of high-risk research materials.

DATA MANAGEMENT

Archival systems, record maintenance, database administration, and documentation protocols for research materials.

FIELD OPERATIONS

Investigation teams, incident response in the field, direct observation of behavioral anomalies, and on-site documentation.
